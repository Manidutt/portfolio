About

I am a dedicated educator and technologist with over 5 years of experience in teaching computer science, specializing in full-stack development, AI, and mobile app development. As a Ph.D. scholar at Karnavati University, I am researching AI-driven educational tools, including my Virtual Teacher Application project.

My career includes teaching at different Colleges/universities, and mentoring interns at ARTPARK (IISc, Bengaluru), and leading coding initiatives like the 30 Days Code Challenge. I hold double Master degrees in Computer Application, and also complemented by many certifications in education and AI by Google, Intel, Microsoft etc.

  • Expertise in Python, Java, PHP, Kotlin, and AI/ML integration etc.
  • Mentored 120+ students in Android app development.

My mission is to bridge academia and industry with hands-on learning and innovative solutions. Current projects include 'Rainbow,' a generative AI tool, and a Virtual Teacher Application to enhance classroom engagement.
